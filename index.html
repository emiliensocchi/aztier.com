About AzTier

This is a simple frontend for the Azure Administrative Tiering (AzTier) project.

Each asset is assigned to a tier based on known attack paths towards full tenant takeover. Lower tier = closer to Global Admin. Direct paths reach Global Admin with the asset alone; indirect paths require pivoting through another identity or resource.
